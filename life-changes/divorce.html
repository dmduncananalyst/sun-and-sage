Divorce

Housing information for property decisions during divorce.

Mortgage Planning

Who gets the house in a divorce if both names are on the mortgage?

How the house is divided, through sale, buyout, or continued co-ownership, is typically decided as part of the divorce settlement, and both spouses remain financially liable on the mortgage until it's formally refinanced.

Divorce Planning

Do I have to sell my house if I'm getting divorced?

No, alternatives include one spouse buying out the other's equity through a refinance, or in some cases continued joint ownership for a period of time.

Learn More →
Refinancing

Can I refinance the house to buy out my ex-spouse?

Yes, this requires the remaining spouse to qualify independently for a new loan covering the existing balance plus the buyout amount for the other spouse's equity share.

Learn More →
Home Equity

How is home equity split in a divorce?

Community property states generally split equity built during the marriage 50/50, while equitable distribution states divide it based on what a court considers fair given the specific circumstances.

Learn More →
Mortgage Planning

What happens to the mortgage if my ex-spouse moves out?

Moving out doesn't remove either spouse's legal responsibility for the mortgage; only a formal refinance or loan assumption legally releases a departing spouse from the debt.

Learn More →
Affordability

Can I afford my house on one income after a divorce?

This requires recalculating the mortgage payment against a single income and total post-divorce costs, which often reveals the need to refinance, sell, or adjust the budget.

Learn More →
Divorce Planning

Should I sell the house or keep it during a divorce?

This depends on affordability, emotional attachment, children's stability, and each spouse's post-divorce financial goals, there's no universally correct answer.

Learn More →
Divorce Planning

How long do I have to sell the house after a divorce settlement?

There's no universal legal deadline, timelines depend on what's specified in the settlement itself, commonly 30 to 180 days unless otherwise negotiated.

Refinancing

What credit score do I need to refinance after a divorce?

There is no single credit-score requirement for every refinance after divorce.

Moving Timeline

Is it better to sell the house before or after the divorce is final?

Selling before finalization can simplify the settlement, while waiting allows more preparation time, and the better choice depends on the couple's specific circumstances.
