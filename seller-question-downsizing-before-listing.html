I've lived in my house for years and I'm ready for something smaller. What should I figure out before I put it on the market?

Figure out where you want to go, what your current home may realistically sell for, what you may net, and how much preparation or downsizing you want to do before the move. Having the replacement plan and the sale plan mapped out together can prevent the process from feeling rushed.

Decide where you are going first

Think about whether you want to stay in the same part of the Valley, move closer to family, reduce maintenance, change home type, or make a larger lifestyle change. The replacement-home plan affects how the sale should be timed.

Understand the current home and likely proceeds

Review the home’s current value, needed repairs, likely preparation costs, and an estimated seller net sheet. That gives you a clearer picture of what the downsizing move can realistically support.

Plan the physical move too

Decluttering, storage, donations, repairs, and deciding what furniture will fit the next home can take longer than expected. Starting early keeps the sale from becoming a rushed packing project.

California and San Fernando Valley context

San Fernando Valley buyers may respond differently to cosmetic updates depending on price range, neighborhood, and competing inventory. Before spending heavily, compare the home's current condition with nearby listings and recent sales so repairs and presentation work are prioritized by likely market impact.
