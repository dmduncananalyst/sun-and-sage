How Much Down Payment Do I Need to Buy a Home?

Your required down payment depends on the loan, the home, and your qualifications. Compare a few cash contributions, then include closing costs and the savings you want to keep after moving.

Separate the down payment from other costs

The down payment reduces the amount borrowed. Closing costs, prepaid expenses, and your reserve funds are separate parts of the budget. Ask for a written estimate that shows each item.
