VA IRRRL

Financing structure

Generally no equity cash withdrawal; allowable costs may be financed, subject to VA rules.

Borrower and property eligibility

Existing VA loan refinance with required recoupment, seasoning and benefit tests.

Underwriting and application

Compare old and new rate, term, payment, funding fee and recoupment; lender may require extra documentation.

Program details, costs and financing example

An interest rate reduction refinance loan generally replaces an existing VA loan. The borrower certifies prior occupancy rather than necessarily current occupancy. The lender must satisfy applicable seasoning, rate reduction and recoupment requirements.

The usual funding fee is 0.5% unless exempt. A $400,000 balance produces $2,000 fee before other eligible costs. Compare the new payment, remaining term and total financed charges; a lower rate alone does not establish savings. Ask whether the lender requires credit review or appraisal despite the streamlined VA route.

Refinance economics and restrictions This product refinances existing VA debt rather than providing general cash proceeds. The VA handbook permits certain closing expenses to be financed, including qualifying funding fees, but limits financed discount points to two.

For example, $6,000 of refinance costs divided by $150 monthly savings equals a simple 40 month recovery period. That is an economic illustration, not the statutory recoupment test. Examine applicable seasoning, benefit and recoupment requirements before signing; a lower payment caused solely by a longer term can increase lifetime interest.

Loan structure and qualification details

The lender must evaluate the applicable seasoning and net benefit rules. An ARM to fixed refinance is not evaluated exactly like a fixed to fixed transaction.

Financing and cost analysis

At $400,000 balance, a $2,000 funding fee and $3,000 other financed charges would raise debt to $405,000 if permitted. Compare savings against the additional principal rather than treating financing as free.

Benefits, limitations and lender review

Useful for eligible existing VA borrowers seeking improved terms, but not for withdrawing equity. Verify recoupment using the lender's regulatory calculation, not merely a consumer break even estimate.

Rate reduction and recoupment

An IRRRL refinances an existing VA mortgage. Prior occupancy certification may be sufficient. VA seasoning, rate reduction and recoupment conditions apply, and the new loan is not designed for unrestricted equity withdrawal.

Qualification, documentation and lending considerations

Compare old and new principal, interest rate, payment, remaining term and funding fee. Some lenders require additional verification even where VA does not require a full appraisal. Obtain the applicable statutory recoupment calculation, not merely a sales illustration.

Financing example and practical risks

A $350,000 loan with a 0.5% applicable fee adds $1,750 before other costs. If qualifying costs are $5,400 and monthly savings are $200, simple recovery is 27 months. Actual VA recoupment excludes or includes costs according to its rules.

Recoupment and financed costs

Compare existing and proposed rate, remaining amortization, new principal and required payment benefit. The statutory recoupment test has its own definition of costs and savings; it is not interchangeable with a simple household break even estimate. Ask for the VA comparison disclosure and confirm seasoning, prior occupancy certification and any funding fee exemption.
