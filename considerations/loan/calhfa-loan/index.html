CalHFA Loan

California Housing Finance Agency programs combine eligible first mortgages with financing options that may reduce cash needed at closing.

Eligibility and property requirements

Program specific income, occupancy, property, credit and homebuyer education rules apply. Many assistance programs require first time buyer status. Apply through a participating lender and verify current availability.

Advantages

May expand access to homeownership for qualifying California households.

Disadvantages and risks

Funding, income limits and repayment terms can restrict access. Assistance is not necessarily a grant.

Interest rates and borrowing costs

Compare the combined cost of the first mortgage and any subordinate financing, not just the advertised first mortgage rate.

Practical considerations

A qualified Los Angeles County buyer with limited savings can ask a participating lender whether a CalHFA first mortgage and MyHome assistance can be combined.

Financing structure

CalHFA first mortgages and subordinate assistance are separate obligations. A participating lender determines which combinations are available. MyHome assistance and other programs have distinct rules, and funding availability can change.

Underwriting and documentation

Review current income limits, first time buyer definition, credit and debt requirements, homebuyer education and occupancy. Obtain the lender's program checklist and complete education before the required deadline. A property must qualify under both the first mortgage and assistance rules.

Illustrative financing calculation

If a $600,000 purchase requires $21,000 down and a hypothetical assistance loan provides $15,000, the remaining down payment is $6,000 before closing costs. Deferred payments do not eliminate the balance. Sale, refinance or transfer may trigger repayment according to the note.

Transaction planning and risk

Compare the combined financing against a mortgage without assistance. Request written terms for repayment, subordination and any appreciation sharing. Confirm reservation and rate lock deadlines before committing to a closing date.

Loan options

CalHFA FHA: FHA first mortgage through approved CalHFA lender; eligible assistance is separate debt. CalHFA Conventional: Conforming first mortgage; possible mortgage insurance and compatible subordinate assistance. MyHome: Deferred payment junior loan, not an automatic grant; amount and repayment triggers depend on current program.

Loan structure and qualification details

CalHFA works through approved lenders and combines first mortgage financing with eligible assistance. The first mortgage has its own underwriting and the assistance has separate eligibility and repayment terms. A reservation is important because funds and pricing may change.

Financing and cost analysis

On a $550,000 home a 3% conventional down payment is $16,500. A hypothetical $15,000 junior loan could reduce that contribution to $1,500 before closing costs, but total secured debt increases by $15,000.

Benefits, limitations and lender review

Compare the first mortgage rate with an ordinary FHA or conventional quote. Review income limits, education, prior ownership, approved lender, reservation and junior lien payoff rules. Do not assume all programs are open simultaneously.

California first mortgages and assistance

CalHFA works through approved lenders and offers conventional and government backed first mortgage options. Assistance is tied to compatible first mortgages; a buyer cannot assume that any junior loan can be combined with any lender product.

Qualification, documentation and lending considerations

County income limits, education, credit rules and fund reservation matter. Compare the standard first mortgage against CalPLUS Access and other available options using the current program handbook. A higher rate can offset the benefit of reduced closing cash. Assistance can create a second or third lien.

Financing example and practical risks

For a $550,000 purchase with $16,500 down and $12,000 costs, a hypothetical $15,000 assistance loan lowers initial cash from $28,500 to $13,500, before credits. It does not erase the $15,000 debt. Model net proceeds at resale and ask about subordination if refinancing.

Additional financing options

Dream For All Shared Appreciation Loan: California assistance for eligible first generation homebuyers.

CalPLUS Access Conventional: California conventional first mortgage with compatible assistance.

CalHFA VA Loan: California state program first mortgage using VA financing.

CalHFA USDA Loan: California program financing using USDA eligible rural housing mortgages.

State program conditions

The state housing agency works through approved lenders and publishes program-specific matrices. First mortgage eligibility, homebuyer education, income limits, funding reservations and junior assistance compatibility must be checked together. A rate quote is not a reservation. The underlying FHA, conventional, VA or USDA rules still apply. Compare the total payment and the payoff of all subordinate liens at a future sale.
