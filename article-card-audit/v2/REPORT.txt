REMAINING SPACE: CAUSE AND CORRECTION
The previous row-sizing correction left entire excerpts unbounded. Long previews, including supplementary blog mobile paragraphs, could still enlarge a row and leave its shorter neighbors empty. Three tablet columns also made full headlines wrap excessively.
The complete preview-copy container now displays at most four lines; the original text remains in the HTML. Headlines stay complete and unclamped. Rows remain content-driven and cards stretch only within their own row, with margin-top:auto on Read More. Body padding is 18px vertically. Tablet grids use two columns from 601-950px; tested desktop widths retain three, and mobile retains one. Measured excerpt-to-link distance is 31-92px across the 75 cases, including normal body/footer padding and the modest space needed to align cards with different full-title lengths. Mobile distances are about 35px. No text or image assets were rewritten.
