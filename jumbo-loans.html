Jumbo Loans and Conforming Loan Limits

A jumbo mortgage may be needed when the amount borrowed exceeds the applicable conforming limit for the county and property type. A high purchase price alone does not decide this, because the down payment affects the loan amount.

Use the limit for the property

Conforming loan limits depend on the year, county, and number of units. Compare the amount borrowed with the current Federal Housing Finance Agency limit for the property rather than using the national baseline alone.

Compare lender requirements

Loans above the applicable conforming limit may use jumbo financing. Down payment, reserves, credit, and pricing vary by lender. Ask for the exact qualification and reserve requirements before making an offer.

California and San Fernando Valley context

For a San Fernando Valley purchase, the lender determines eligibility and loan terms, while the property can add costs such as taxes, HOA dues, insurance, repairs, and appraisal related issues. Compare written lender disclosures with the full cost of the specific home.

Understanding Written Loan Terms

Compare financing using the written terms for your application and property. The loan amount, repayment period, rate structure, mortgage insurance, lender charges, and cash required at closing work together. A change in one part of the offer can affect the others, so a quoted monthly principal and interest payment is only part of the comparison.

Ask which conditions remain before approval and what changes require a new review. New debt, a change in employment or documented income, or a property issue can affect financing before closing. Use the lender’s current disclosures and explanations rather than relying on a general program description as a promise of eligibility or final terms.

Looking Beyond an Approval Amount

A lender evaluates whether a mortgage meets its underwriting requirements, while your household budget also includes expenses that may not appear in that calculation. Consider utilities, transportation, healthcare, childcare, maintenance, and the savings you want to preserve. A payment can meet a lending rule and still feel uncomfortable in daily life.

Use current information for the particular property, including taxes, insurance, association dues, and any known repairs. Leave room for changes rather than assuming that every expense will stay fixed. Comparing the full monthly picture and the cash remaining after a move makes it easier to recognize the difference between a qualified purchase and a sustainable one.
