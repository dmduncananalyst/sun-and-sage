Can I Use One Loan to Buy and Renovate a Home?

Yes. Some renovation mortgages combine a home purchase with eligible improvements in one loan. The property, work, contractor, and borrower must meet the selected program’s requirements.

Plan the work before the offer

Define the proposed repairs and ask the lender which renovation program fits. A contractor’s scope and cost estimate help the lender evaluate the work and the proposed completed value.

Make room for the process

Renovation financing includes additional review and work completion requirements. Coordinate the financing timeline, permits, contractor availability, and any temporary housing before committing to the property.
