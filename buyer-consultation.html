Buyer Consultation

A home search that starts with you.

Your Buyer Consultation

A buyer consultation is time to clarify what you want to buy, what you’re comfortable spending, and when you’d like to move. You can be early in the process or ready to tour. Together, you’ll shape a search around your life and priorities.

Your home and your area

Talk about the space you need, the neighborhoods you’re considering, and the details you don’t want to compromise on.

Your budget and financing

Discuss a comfortable price range, the costs to plan for, and whether an introduction to a lender would help.

Your timing and the buying process

Walk through showings, offers, inspections, and the decisions that come with a purchase, at a pace that fits your situation.
