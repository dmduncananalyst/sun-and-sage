Market perspective

A closer look at Tujunga

Market data current as of August 2026. Home prices shift monthly.

Market snapshot

Tujunga's median home value is approximately $890K to $950K (often tracked jointly with Sunland as "Sunland-Tujunga"), with price per square foot around $644. The market is somewhat competitive, with homes typically selling in around 43 to 50 days.

Life in Tujunga

Schools

Schools in Tujunga

Tujunga is served by LAUSD. Buyers should verify attendance boundaries for the exact property.

Commute + transit

Getting around from Tujunga

SR-210 is the main freeway connection, with Foothill Boulevard serving as the principal local commercial route. Most commuting is car-oriented.

Shopping + dining

Everyday shopping and dining

Foothill Boulevard provides local restaurants, groceries, retail, and everyday services, while Glendale and La Crescenta offer larger shopping areas nearby.

Parks + recreation

Parks and outdoor access

McGroarty Arts Center, nearby Sunland-Tujunga parks, and extensive Angeles National Forest access make outdoor recreation a major part of the area.

Healthcare

Nearby hospitals and medical access

USC Verdugo Hills Hospital is one of the closest major hospital options.

HOA costs

HOA costs in Tujunga

Most established single family homes have no HOA. Condos, townhomes, and newer small lot developments may have dues roughly in the $300-$600 monthly range.

Property taxes

Property tax considerations

Property taxes depend on assessed value and property-specific assessments. Buyers should use the exact tax record for the property.

Insurance + hazards

Hazard and insurance considerations

Wildfire risk is a major consideration because Tujunga sits at the base of the San Gabriel Mountains. Buyers should review fire-zone status, evacuation routes, brush clearance, and insurance availability.
