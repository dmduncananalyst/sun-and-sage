Marketing

Bring your home into focus, from its first introduction to the details buyers take with them.

Coming Soon + MarketingSignagePrinted CollateralSearch MarketingSocial Media StrategyEmail MarketingTargeted Digital AdvertisingMagazine + Newspaper Features
01

Coming Soon+ Marketing

A strong launch starts before the property is public. Dominique organizes the photography, listing details, showing plan, campaign materials, and buyer communication so the home feels considered from the first look.

The goal is a polished introduction that gives the property room to stand out while keeping the seller’s timing and the required listing rules in view.

02

Signage

Well placed signage gives the property a clear presence in its neighborhood. A consistent design connects the on-site experience with the listing’s photographs, digital materials, and showing information.

Placement and timing are coordinated with the owner and local requirements.

03

Printed Collateral

Thoughtful printed pieces keep the home’s story clear after a showing. The materials are selected to fit the property and the agreed campaign.

Open house invitations

A clear introduction to the event and the home.

Postcards

A concise property story for neighborhood outreach.

Newsletters

Property highlights within a broader market conversation.

Flyers

Essential information buyers can keep after a visit.

Brochures

Photography and details brought into one refined presentation.

Upscale Lookbook

A more expansive printed story when the home calls for it.

04

Search Marketing

Buyers need to find accurate information when they search. Dominique coordinates the listing details, photography, and property description, and reviews how the home is presented across the selected listing channels.

Distribution depends on the MLS and each platform. Paid placements, when selected, are part of the agreed campaign.

Social Media Strategy

Use a consistent visual story to introduce the home, highlight the details, and invite a closer look. Short videos, photographs, and clear descriptions give each platform content that suits the way people use it.

06

Email Marketing

A focused email can introduce a property, share a new opportunity, or keep interested buyers and agents informed as the listing progresses.

Photography, useful details, and a clear invitation to view the home keep each message easy to understand. Communications are sent to appropriate, permission-based contacts.

07

Targeted Digital Advertising

When included in the marketing plan, paid campaigns can extend the property’s reach beyond its existing audience. Creative, placement, budget, and timing are selected together.

Campaign activity is reviewed against the agreed goals, with adjustments guided by the response.

08

Magazine+ Newspaper Features

Some homes benefit from a longer visual story. A considered print placement or editorial pitch can bring attention to the architecture, setting, and distinctive details.

Opportunities are evaluated for fit and availability. Publisher acceptance and earned editorial coverage are not guaranteed.
