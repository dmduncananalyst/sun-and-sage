Touring Homes

Tour with a clear plan so each showing helps you decide what fits and what does not.

Before You Tour

Review your price range, location, must-haves, and deal breakers before you arrive. That gives you something useful to compare at every showing.

At the Home

Look at the layout, natural light, storage, condition, street, and neighborhood. Pay attention to the things that would be difficult or expensive to change.

After the Showing

Compare the home with the others you have seen while the details are still fresh. Dominique helps you separate a strong fit from a home that simply photographed well.
