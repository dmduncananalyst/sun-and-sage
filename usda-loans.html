Can I Use a USDA Loan to Buy a Home With No Down Payment?

Yes, eligible borrowers and eligible properties may qualify for USDA guaranteed financing with no down payment. Both household income and the property’s location must meet program requirements. Loan terms and eligibility vary by lender and borrower, so compare the written Loan Estimate, total monthly payment, cash needed at closing, and ongoing costs.

Start with the address

Do not assume a home qualifies because it is near a rural area. Have the lender check the exact address using USDA’s current eligibility tools before you plan your financing around this program.

Then check the household and loan

Income limits vary by area and household size. The lender also reviews repayment ability and property requirements. Zero down does not eliminate closing costs or applicable program fees.

California and San Fernando Valley context

For a San Fernando Valley purchase, the lender determines eligibility and loan terms, while the property can add costs such as taxes, HOA dues, insurance, repairs, and appraisal-related issues. Compare written lender disclosures with the full cost of the specific home.
