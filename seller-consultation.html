Your home, your timing, your priorities.

Let’s Sell Your Home Together

Your Seller Consultation

A seller consultation is a chance to review your property with Dominique and work through the decisions involved in selling it. You’ll discuss your goals, the home’s condition, current market activity, and the timing that makes sense for you.

Your property and its position

Review the home’s features, condition, and comparable market activity to inform a thoughtful pricing discussion.

Preparation and presentation

Consider which repairs, updates, staging, and photography would support your sale, along with the scope and costs before work begins.

The sale, from launch to closing

Review marketing, showings, offers, and how your sale could fit with a move or another home purchase.

What to Bring

Share the property address, any updates you’ve made, and when you’re considering selling. You don’t need a finished home or a firm moving date to begin.
