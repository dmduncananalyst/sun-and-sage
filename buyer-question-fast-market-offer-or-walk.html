Homes I like keep selling fast. How do I know when to make an offer and when to walk away?

Decide your price range, must-haves, and deal breakers before a fast-moving listing creates pressure. Move quickly when the home and terms still fit your plan, and walk away when the price, condition, risk, or compromises cross limits you set in advance.

Know your limits before the house appears

Write down your maximum comfortable price, important contingencies, location needs, condition standards, and non-negotiables before you fall in love with a particular property.

Use the property facts, not just the competition

Review comparable sales, disclosures, known condition issues, likely monthly cost, and the offer terms you would need to accept. A multiple-offer situation does not automatically make a home worth more to you.

Walking away can be part of a good buying strategy

If the numbers stop working, material concerns are unresolved, or winning requires terms you are not comfortable with, passing can protect your larger goal. Another property will come along.

California and San Fernando Valley context

The San Fernando Valley includes both fast-moving pockets and homes that need more negotiation or due diligence. Dominique can help you compare the real-estate side of the decision while inspectors and lenders address their licensed areas.
