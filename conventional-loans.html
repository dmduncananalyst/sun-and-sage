Can I Get a Conventional Loan Without Putting 20% Down?

Yes. Some conventional loans allow less than 20% down for eligible buyers. A smaller down payment can bring mortgage insurance and a different monthly cost, so compare the complete loan offer. Loan terms and eligibility vary by lender and borrower, so compare the written Loan Estimate, total monthly payment, cash needed at closing, and ongoing costs.

What changes with your down payment

A conventional mortgage is not part of a government insurance program. The down payment, property type, credit history, income, and lender’s underwriting all affect the available options. Ask a lender which programs apply to your situation.

Compare the whole picture

Set your comfortable cash contribution first. Then compare the interest rate, mortgage insurance, cash needed at closing, and the savings you would have left after moving.
