Concierge Services

Prepare each space, bring the presentation together, and enter the market with a plan.

BEFORE YOUR HOME GOES ON THE MARKET

Preparation with a purpose.

Presentation, photography, pricing, and showings, coordinated around your property.

Property ReviewHome Improvement ServicesStagingFloor Plans + Photographic RenderingsPhotographyPricing + StrategyOpen Houses + Private Showings
01

Property Review

Start with a walkthrough and a practical conversation about the home. Dominique reviews its condition, layout, and presentation, then helps you prioritize the improvements that fit your timing and budget.

You leave with a clear sequence for preparation, from small finishing details to the work that needs a specialist.

02

Home Improvement Services

Focus the work before committing to the spend. Dominique can help coordinate qualified providers and compare the scope with your selling goals.

  • Decluttering
  • Deep cleaning
  • Painting
  • Landscaping
  • Floor care
  • Lighting updates
  • Repairs
  • Kitchen and bath refreshes

Service scope, provider availability, and costs are agreed before work begins.

03

Staging

A thoughtful furniture arrangement makes the layout easy to understand. Dominique considers scale, lighting, and circulation so each room is presented clearly and comfortably.

Dominique’s design perspective helps shape a presentation that feels inviting in person and in photographs.

04

Floor Plans+ Photographic Renderings

Clear floor plans help buyers understand room relationships, circulation, and the overall layout. When appropriate, photographic renderings can illustrate possibilities that are difficult to see in an unfinished space.

Plans and visualizations are commissioned for the property as needed. Renderings are labeled, and buyers should verify measurements and feasibility independently.

Pricing+ Strategy

Recent comparable sales, the home’s condition, and competing listings help shape a realistic price recommendation.

Dominique reviews the evidence with you, then follows showing activity and buyer feedback as the market responds. The final asking price is your decision.

07

Open Houses+ Private Showings

Make every visit feel considered. Dominique coordinates access, prepares the home for appointments, and brings the conversation back to the details buyers need to make a decision.

  1. Agree on availability and access
  2. Prepare the home before visits
  3. Share property information
  4. Follow up and review feedback

A home that’s ready to be seen.
