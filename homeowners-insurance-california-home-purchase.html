Homeowners Insurance When Purchasing a California Home

November 27, 2026

A buyer may find a home that fits the purchase budget but encounter difficulty arranging homeowners insurance. It is worth investigating coverage early rather than waiting until the closing date approaches.

Compare coverage, not only premiums

Ask a licensed insurance professional about dwelling coverage, personal property, liability, deductibles and exclusions. The amount needed to rebuild a home is not necessarily its purchase price. Flood and earthquake coverage generally require separate arrangements.

Insurance availability in California

Availability and underwriting can vary by property. Location, construction features and condition may affect the options a buyer receives. If standard coverage is unavailable, discuss the California FAIR Plan and any additional coverage needed with a licensed insurance professional. A FAIR Plan policy is not equivalent to a comprehensive homeowners policy.

Coordinate with your lender and purchase contract

A lender may require evidence of coverage before closing. Ask about insurance requirements and discuss relevant contract deadlines with your real estate agent. Obtain quotes for the specific property rather than assuming another home's premium will apply.

Plan for ongoing ownership

Consider the deductible, coverage limitations and future renewal uncertainty along with the monthly mortgage payment. Keep insurance documents available and review coverage when the property changes.
